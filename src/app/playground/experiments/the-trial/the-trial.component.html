The Charges

  • Count 1 — Spoke harsh words.
  • Count 2 — Implied she is "not smart."
  • Count 3 — Implied she is "not good enough."

All counts are admitted. The defense will not contest the facts.

🙏

The Plea

Mawuli Badassou stands and addresses the bench:

"Your Honor — guilty. On every count. I have no defense to mount.

I only ask that the court hear my case before sentencing. Not so I can be excused. So that you know that I know what I did."

plea recorded: guilty 🙏

💭

Statement of Facts

Esther — I said things I shouldn't have said.

I knew them as soon as they left my mouth.

I said them anyway, and that part is the problem.


I'm not here to argue I didn't hurt you, Akua.

I did.


I'm here because the things I said about you are demonstrably, factually, on-the-record wrong — and I want this court to hear the evidence. 💌

🔑
Exhibit A

on the matter of being "not smart"

Submitted the shop she works at.

Her Madam taught her, and within a short window she was running the shop herself. Madams do not hand the keys to people who are "not smart."

This court takes judicial notice that the prosecution has, in fact, the brain. 🧠✨

verdict on the charge: not true 💕

🎂
Exhibit B

on the matter of being "not enough"

Submitted birthdays.

She remembers them all. Birthdays, anniversaries, the day my cousin's baby came home. I have a calendar app and still forget. She has a brain and remembers without it.

This is not the memory of someone who is "not enough." This is the memory of someone who notices people. Including me. 💗

verdict on the charge: not true 💕

📦 ✈️
Exhibit C

on the matter of growth

Submitted she is teaching herself importation.

Nobody asked her to. She decided. She is doing it.

Someone "not smart" does not pick up a new trade. Someone "not enough" does not look at the world and decide to add one more skill to themselves. ✨

verdict on the charge: not true 💕

👗 ✨
Exhibit D

on the matter of taste

Submitted how she dresses.

Her fashion sense is its own argument. When she walks in somewhere, the room recalibrates. The defendant has, on several occasions, just looked. 👀

The court will accept good taste as a sign of intelligence. The court has read books. 📚

verdict on the charge: not true 💕

🍲
Exhibit E

on the matter of her hands

Submitted she cooked for me once.

Once was enough. I have not enjoyed a restaurant the same way since. The defendant offers this in evidence not to flatter, but to factually establish that she is good at what she puts her hands on. 🤲
